Logo review — round 3

Your concept: the HR ligature

Two hand-crafted executions of your brief — H and R for Humanoid Robotics, sharing one central stem, white letterforms over a neon teal glow, with chamfered corners and a subtle robot hint that emerges from the letterforms rather than being drawn on top of them. Both come in light and dark variants. (Canva’s credit quota ran out this round — these are all built by hand, which is what a brief this precise needs anyway.)

Concept 1 — HR Vision Hand-built SVG · dual variant

Bold monoline. The H’s crossbar flows in one continuous stroke into the R’s bowl — a true ligature. Place the crossbar high and the two closed counters it creates sit side by side like a visor: two subtle eyes, with the stems below reading as the body. The bowl’s outer corners are chamfered at 45° — the machined, pointed detail. Depth comes from the white-to-mint gradient on the core, the neon underglow, and the R’s leg rendered in teal while everything else stays white.

light variant — ink core, teal glow
dark variant — white core, neon glow
32 px · light + dark variants

Concept 2 — HR Circuit Hand-built SVG · dual variant

Fine traces. The same shared-stem ligature, drawn thin, like traces on a circuit board — with 45° bends and three neon via pads at the joints (the pad on the shared stem is literally where H meets R). The H’s crossbar and the R’s bowl bottom align on one horizontal shoulder line across the mark — the quiet body hint. Deliberate, precise, technical.

light variant — ink traces, teal pads
dark variant — white traces, neon pads
32 px · light + dark variants
How the robotics hint works: nothing is drawn on top of the letters. The face emerges from the letterforms themselves — the two closed counters in Vision sit like a visor with two eyes; in Circuit the aligned crossbar and bowl read as a shoulder line, and the pads sit at the joints like actuators. Squint and you see HR; look closer and you see the machine. Both are pure SVG (crisp at any size, glow via blur filter) and both switch colors with the site theme via logo: { src, srcDark }.
